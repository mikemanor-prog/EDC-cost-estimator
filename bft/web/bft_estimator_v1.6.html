Model: I = k x derate x A0.5 x p0.39 x (rise / 30)0.61, with A the area (mm2) and p the perimeter (mm). k = 5.95 is an approximate fit to DIN 43671 bare copper ratings. Calibrate it on the Assumptions tab.
