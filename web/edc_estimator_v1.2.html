Molding

Cycle time = base + k × wall². Rule-of-thumb clamp (US t) = projected area (in²) × cavities × (1 + runner area %) × tons/in². This is the same as the metric form tonnes = area (cm²) × pressure (kgf/cm²) ÷ 1000 × cavities; 1 US t/in² = 140.6 kgf/cm². The press used is your entry, or the rule of thumb if blank. Machine rate comes from the band of the press used unless you override it. Molding cost per part = (machine + labor × operators) × cycle ÷ cavities. Material = part mass × (1 + runner volume %) × $/kg. Runner % comes from each part's gate / runner type. Band rates are North American placeholders; replace them with Durango rates.
